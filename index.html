the game

Faans value a hand. Points decide the game.

MahPoints plays traditional Hong Kong-style mahjong, with 3 faans minimum, flowers, dealers, limit hands and the familiar hand-completion rules.

We do not shorten your game to fit a timeframe, nor reduce it to a faan-only format. Every time you attend, you continue from where you left off.

Full rules and scoring details will be provided before play. See the rules.

MahPoints is for you if you want to

  • Play Hong Kong mahjong authentically, with faans and points
  • Meet fellow players for regular games
  • Find peers of similar skill level to play with
  • Complete games of 16 hands, over multiple sessions
  • Track your points to strategize
  • Improve your skills over time
  • Celebrate winners on the leaderboard

a night out

  • Arrive and check in

    Check in, take your seat, and place your dim sum order, included with your ticket.

  • Play the full game

    A scribe logs each hand and transfers points as you play. Enjoy your dim sum without stopping the game.

  • Tea and additional orders

    Unlimited tea is included. You are welcome to purchase additional food and drinks.

  • Skill-aware tables

    We endeavour to organize tables by experience and, as the league develops, by ELO. Let us know whether you prefer Swift or Zen pace in your MahPoints profile, and we will do our best to accommodate.

  • Points and ELO

    Your results carry forward from session to session, with your progress recorded in your personal tracker.

seating

MahPoints uses your experience level and playing preferences to build compatible tables. Seating is organized on a best-effort basis. Earlier registrations receive priority when accommodating preferences.

Standard and Apprentice players play on separate tables, subject to the final mix of registrants.

If four of you want to sit together, let us know as soon as you receive your tickets.

good to know

  • No gambling

    No bets. All forms of gambling are strictly prohibited.

  • Keep tables clean

    No food or drinks on mahjong tabletops. In case of any equipment damage, you agree to pay for replacement.

  • Code of conduct

    MahPoints is a welcoming, respectful, and inclusive community. Harassment, discrimination, cheating, intimidation or disruptive behaviour will not be tolerated.

  • Refunds and cancellations

    Late arrivals or cancellations impact an entire table, so please treat your registration as a commitment to your table.

    • Cancel at least 3 days before the event: eligible for a refund, less applicable processing fees.
    • Within 3 days of the event: tickets are non-refundable.
    • No-shows: not eligible for refunds or credits.
    • Tickets may be transferred to another player who meets the requirements for your division. Contact us as soon as possible if you need to make a change.

  • Rules of play

    The official MahPoints rules and scoring sheet provided for the event govern play. Players are expected to follow organizer instructions and venue rules.

  • Photography

    Photography and video may take place during events for promotional and social-media purposes. Please let the organizer know if you do not wish to be photographed.
